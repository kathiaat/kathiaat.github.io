Project 01 · Editorial

Editorial Series

A six-issue print series exploring craft, slowness, and the rituals around making.

Role
Art Direction · Layout
Year
2025
Format
Print · 240 × 320mm

Built from a single grid and two type families, the series uses generous white space and considered typography to slow the reader down. Each issue takes a different lens — material, place, voice — but follows the same editorial rhythm.

Photography commissioned across three cities, with each contributor framed within the same minimal typographic system. The covers rotate through a palette pulled from the season's commissioned imagery.

Project 02 · Photography

Quiet Hours

A photo essay on early-morning interiors and the soft hour just before a room wakes up.

Role
Photography · Edit
Year
2025
Output
Web · Zine

Shot over four weeks in three apartments, the work is interested in light as a kind of evidence — what was here yesterday, what's still warming up, what hasn't been touched yet today.

The accompanying zine pairs the photographs with short, fragmentary captions in a typewriter sans, printed at A5 on uncoated stock.

Project 03 · 3D · Motion

Iridescent Studies

A set of looping 3D studies exploring chromatic surfaces, soft shadows, and quiet motion.

Role
3D · Direction
Year
2024
Tools
Blender · Octane

Begun as a single-frame experiment, the studies grew into a small series of looping animations now used as visual seeds across brand work.

Each loop is rendered at 6 seconds, with motion held below 1Hz to keep the work meditative rather than insistent. The accompanying audio is a soft drone built in Ableton.

Project 04 · Lifestyle

Coastal Notes

A travel diary documenting six small towns along the Pacific coast, in image and text.

Role
Photography · Writing
Year
2024
Output
Web · Print

Notes from the road — local breakfasts, peripheral conversations, and a slowly forming map of how a place introduces itself when you stop trying to rush it.

Published as a small site and a 64-page riso-printed booklet, the project's typography borrows from regional fishing-fleet signage I started collecting along the way.

Project 05 · Identity

Field Marks

A flexible identity for a small natural-dye studio, built around marks left by hand.

Role
Identity · Print
Year
2023
Scope
Mark · Type · Packaging

Built from scanned brushwork and a custom monoline display, the system flexes across packaging, swatch cards, and a small e-commerce site without ever needing a heavy logo.

Color is pulled directly from the studio's seasonal dye runs. Each release rotates the palette while keeping the typography and grid steady — the brand is the rhythm, not the colors.
